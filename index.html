02 / Publication

Published research

A sequence-based deep learning framework for accurate antifungal peptide identification.

Journal article

iAFP-fLRM: Accurate identification of antifungal peptides via hybrid deep learning architecture and multi-modal feature fusion

Shengli Zhang, Jianwei Cheng, Guixu Zhou, Jianhua Wang

Biophysical Chemistry 332 (2026) 107591

iAFP-fLRM combines evolutionary BLOSUM62 features, token and position embeddings, adaptive dual-branch fusion, LSTM, and ResMLP classification to identify antifungal peptides directly from amino acid sequences.

03 / Projects

Ongoing work

Optimization-based signal design for efficient sensing and communication systems.

OCDM / Single-Carrier Signal Optimization

Signal processing

An ongoing study of OCDM waveform optimization using the Majorization-Minimization algorithm. The objective is to formulate waveforms with low integrated sidelobe level and peak-to-average power ratio, supported by MATLAB simulations for waveform shaping and sidelobe suppression.
