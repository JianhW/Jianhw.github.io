OCDM / Single-Carrier Signal Optimization

📡 Signal processing

An ongoing study of OCDM waveform optimization using the Majorization-Minimization algorithm. The objective is to formulate waveforms with low integrated sidelobe level and peak-to-average power ratio, supported by MATLAB simulations for waveform shaping and sidelobe suppression.
