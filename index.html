Research Experience & Projects

Compositional Zero-Shot Learning · Project Lead, First Author
Mar. 2026 – Present
Provincial Innovation and Entrepreneurship Training Program · Funded

Developed PRISM to extend primitive sharing from offline representation learning to unlabeled test streams. Evaluated compositional recognition in large-scale open-world and long-tailed settings and introduced Adaptive PRISM-Lite to reduce repeated candidate evaluation.

Deep Learning for Antifungal Peptide Identification · Core Member (1/3)
Dec. 2024 – Nov. 2025
Provincial Innovation and Entrepreneurship Training Program · Rated Excellent

Contributed to amino acid sequence fusion and screening, implementation, and manuscript preparation. Combined Transformer, CNN, LSTM, and BLOSUM62 features to predict antifungal activity from peptide sequences, achieving 95.02% accuracy .

Integrated Radar-Communication Waveform Design · Core Member (1/5)
Apr. 2025 – Present

Developed a joint optimization formulation for periodic/aperiodic integrated sidelobe levels and peak-to-average power ratio (PAPR), subject to fixed communication-symbol constraints. Derived a two-stage majorization–minimization algorithm with closed-form updates.

Across 1,000 Monte Carlo simulations, mean PAPR decreased from 6.76 dB to 2.46 dB while preserving bit-error-rate performance close to the original OCDM waveform.
