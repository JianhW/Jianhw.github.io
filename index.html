Hi there 👋 Welcome to my Homepage!

I am an undergraduate student in Electronic Engineering at Xidian University. I explore how signal processing, optimization, and artificial intelligence can work together to solve real engineering problems.

My current interests span computer vision, joint radar-communication waveform design, and efficient optimization methods. I enjoy connecting mathematical ideas with reproducible simulations and learning-based systems. Find code and experiments on GitHub.

Signal processing OCDM / Single-Carrier Signal Optimization

An ongoing study of OCDM waveform optimization using the Majorization-Minimization algorithm. The objective is to formulate waveforms with low integrated sidelobe level and peak-to-average power ratio, supported by MATLAB simulations for waveform shaping and sidelobe suppression.
